Question 14 of 35 // ID: 20491
A 28-year-old medical student is participating in a marathon on a hot day. After running 20 km without hydration, which of the following changes in extracellular fluid (ECF) and intracellular fluid (ICF) osmolarity and volume will be observed?
Increased ECF volume, decreased ICF osmolarity 1
Increased ECF osmolarity, decreased ICF volume (Hyperosmotic dehydration) 2
Decreased ECF osmolarity, increased ICF volume 3
Iso-osmotic contraction with no shift in ICF volume 4
[RATIONALE]
Sweat excretion is hypotonic. Loss of free water elevates ECF osmolarity, pulling water from the intracellular compartment (ICF). Result: ICF volume decreases and osmolarity rises in both compartments.
