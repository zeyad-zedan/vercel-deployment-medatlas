A 28-year-old medical student is participating in a marathon on a hot day. After running 20 km without hydration, which of the following changes in extracellular fluid (ECF) and intracellular fluid (ICF) osmolarity and volume will be observed?
Increased ECF volume, decreased ICF osmolarity A
Increased ECF osmolarity, decreased ICF volume (Hyperosmotic dehydration) B
Decreased ECF osmolarity, increased ICF volume C
Iso-osmotic contraction with no shift in ICF volume D
Rationale: Hypotonic sweat loss pulls water from ICF to ECF via osmosis. Result: Intracellular volume contracts while both osmolarities rise.
