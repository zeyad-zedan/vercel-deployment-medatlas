[QUESTION 14/35] // LECTURE 01
A 28-year-old medical student is participating in a marathon on a hot day. After running 20 km without hydration, which of the following changes in extracellular fluid (ECF) and intracellular fluid (ICF) osmolarity and volume will be observed?
A
Increased ECF volume, decreased ICF osmolarity
B
Increased ECF osmolarity, decreased ICF volume (Hyperosmotic dehydration)
C
Decreased ECF osmolarity, increased ICF volume
D
Iso-osmotic contraction with no shift in ICF volume
[VERIFIED CLINICAL REASONING]
Sweat is hypotonic fluid loss. Loss of water from ECF draws water out of ICF by osmotic gradient. Result: ICF volume contracts while both compartments' osmolarities rise.
