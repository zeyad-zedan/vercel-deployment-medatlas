Item 14 of 35 · Physiological Hemodynamics
A 28-year-old medical student is participating in a marathon on a hot day. After running 20 km without hydration, which of the following changes in extracellular fluid (ECF) and intracellular fluid (ICF) osmolarity and volume will be observed?
A. Increased ECF volume, decreased ICF osmolarity
B. Increased ECF osmolarity, decreased ICF volume (Hyperosmotic dehydration)
C. Decreased ECF osmolarity, increased ICF volume
D. Iso-osmotic contraction with no shift in ICF volume
Clinical Rationale:
Sweat excretion is characteristically hypotonic relative to systemic plasma. Continued perspiration absent adequate free water intake produces a hyperosmotic contraction of the extracellular compartment. Fluid is consequently mobilized from intracellular stores into the extracellular space along the osmotic gradient, shrinking ICF volume.
